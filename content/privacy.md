---
title: "Privacy"
description: "Privacy notice for this website"
---
This is a personal website, written and maintained by me for my own use and for
anyone who finds it helpful. I do not use analytics or tracking cookies, I do not
collect personal data, and I do not sell or share any information about visitors.
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under
[GitHub's own privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement),
and some scripts load from jsDelivr, code.jquery.com and Cloudflare's cdnjs,
which see your IP address when they serve those files.
Each page embeds a Google Form for comments, so Google can see that you visited
that page even if you do not comment, under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
If you choose to leave a comment, it is submitted through that form, and the
name and text you enter are published on the relevant page; nothing else is
used, and you can ask me to remove a comment at any time.
